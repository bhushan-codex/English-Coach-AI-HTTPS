How to Request Data Deletion

Users of English Coach AI: Speak Better can submit a formal request for the deletion of their personal and practice-related data by sending an email directly to:

Email Address: bhushancodexstudio@gmail.com

Subject Line: Data Deletion Request — English Coach AI


Send Deletion Request Email

Submission Guidelines:

  • In your email, clearly describe your request and identify the specific data you would like deleted.
  • Security Notice: Do not send passwords, credit card numbers, or other sensitive personal authentication credentials in your email message.

Data That May Be Deleted

After verifying and reviewing your request, we will delete all applicable user data that we control, which may include:

  • Voice Recordings: Ephemeral speech clips, if stored or cached by the app or its services.
  • Speech Transcripts & Conversation History: Spoken interaction transcripts and interactive dialogue turns with Coach Alex, if stored.
  • AI Practice Prompts & Learning Activity Records: Prompts, grammar inquiries, and conversational practice logs, if stored.
  • Learning Progress & Practice History: CEFR grade assessment scores, words per minute (WPM) cadence stats, articulation scores, daily practice targets, and streak histories, if stored.
  • Other Associated Personal Information: Any other personal information associated with your request, where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain data categories may be exempted or temporarily retained under specific conditions:

  • Some information may remain temporarily in encrypted disaster-recovery backups, firewall/security logs, or third-party platform service records, where deletion is governed by the relevant provider's retention policies or mandatory legal compliance requirements.
  • If information is processed by an external AI provider (such as Google Gemini API or Firebase services), its retention and deletion lifecycle are also subject to that provider's applicable policies, terms of service, and configured retention settings.

Processing Time

We take privacy and user rights seriously:

  • We will review all incoming deletion requests and confirm receipt via email.
  • Any applicable data under our control will be permanently deleted within a reasonable timeframe, subject to legitimate security requirements and statutory legal obligations.
  • If any retention limitations apply to parts of your requested data, we will clearly communicate them in our response.

Account Information

The Application does not require users to create an account, register with a username, or link a social profile to access language lessons and speaking practice. Consequently, no remote account credentials exist on our servers.

Instant Device-Level Deletion: You can instantly and completely erase all locally stored practice history, audio cache, and settings directly from your Android device at any time:

Open Device Settings > Apps > English Coach AI > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
